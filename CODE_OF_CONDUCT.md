# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We want a technical community that is constructive, respectful, specific,
and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Examples of helpful behavior include:

- explaining disagreement with evidence and curiosity;
- making space for newcomers and crediting contributors;
- reporting failures without shaming the people who found or caused them; and
- protecting private traces, credentials, and security reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private
information, testing systems without authorization, and bad-faith disclosure
of sensitive traces or vulnerabilities.

## Scope and enforcement

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful
content, close conversations, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate,
documented to the extent privacy allows, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact
route listed in the repository settings. Do not use a public issue for a
security report; follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead. Reports should
include the relevant project space, dates, links or context, and any immediate
safety concern. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable.
